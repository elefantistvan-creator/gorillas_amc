Banana war on the rooftops. Take turns and empty the other gorilla's life bar.

Aim (Motion): hold your finger anywhere on the screen — that moment is zero. Turn the phone sideways, away from the target, for power. Nod it (top toward you / away) for height. Lift your finger to throw.

Aim (Touch): put your finger down and drag back like a slingshot; lift to throw. Choose the control in Settings.

The dotted arc shows the path in calm air — judge the wind yourself: the arrow at the top is blue when weak, red when strong.

The harder and longer you pull, the more your hand trembles. You have 10 seconds per turn.

Bananas (bottom): Banana plain · Drill bores through buildings · Split splits into three at the top · Bounce bounces three times · Fire leaves flames that burn for 2 turns · Atom huge blast. Special bananas are earned every time your banana damages the opponent (a hit or a near hit), always in this order: Bounce → Split → Drill → Fire → Atom, then again from the start. They last for the whole match.

Jump ◀ ▶ (green): hop to the next roof before you throw — every turn.

Builds (row above the bananas) take your whole turn and work once per match: Floor+ / Floor− raise or lower the whole roof you stand on · Shield held in your hand, soaks 30% damage (the drill goes through) · Scope next turn the aim arc includes the wind.

Hidden tricks (purple), your opponent doesn't see them: Umbrella catches the next banana (not the drill) · Wind twists the wind for their next throw, while their arrow still shows the old one · Time they get only 4 seconds next turn.

God mode (both players agree): everything unlimited.

Damage: direct hits, near misses, fire, falling. Buildings crumble — shoot the floor away and the gorilla falls.

On a PC: press on the track and drag back like a slingshot.
